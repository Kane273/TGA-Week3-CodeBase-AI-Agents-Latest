# Three-minute ResearchGuard walkthrough

## 0:00–0:25 — Start with the problem

Select a RAG safety or AI-assisted security-review question. Explain that one research question
normally requires disconnected searching, methods design, statistics, ethics review, and manual
revision.

## 0:25–1:05 — Show multi-agent orchestration

Start **Guided showcase** and open Expert view. Point to the LangGraph topology as the planner
dispatches parallel evidence specialists. Name the distinct hypothesis, methods, ethics,
statistics, skeptical-review, revision, and synthesis responsibilities.

## 1:05–1:35 — Show failure and recovery

Highlight the simulated source outage, the recorded tool failure, and the fallback route. Open
one evidence provenance trail to show the source, passage, claim-support score, and verification
result.

## 1:35–2:10 — Show disagreement improving the work

Open the disagreement and before/after views. Show that the skeptical reviewer detects missing
attrition inflation and that the revision agent increases enrollment rather than hiding the
critique.

## 2:10–2:35 — Show human governance

At the approval interrupt, request “Add weekly adverse-event monitoring.” Show the requirement
in the next protocol revision, then approve. Explain that reads are autonomous while consequential
writes require explicit human action.

## 2:35–3:00 — Close with measured evidence

Open Presentation mode and state: “On the committed deterministic benchmark, ResearchGuard
and its credible single-agent baseline both completed 24 of 24 deterministic drafting tasks.
ResearchGuard achieved collaboration value on 24 of 24 because it records specialist critique
and revision; that metric is not applicable to a single-agent system.” Clearly label this as an orchestration benchmark,
then export the report.
